Droplet Sizer

Measure water-in-oil droplet diameters from microscopy images, right in your browser.

Click to upload or drag an image here

PNG / JPG / TIFF microscopy image

1. Calibrate scale

Draw a line along a known reference length (e.g. a scale bar), then enter its real-world length.

If you already know your microscope/camera's calibration (e.g. from a calibration slide or objective spec), enter it directly. This stays applied across images you upload afterward.

2. Detect droplets

Set the expected diameter/spacing range for your droplets, tune the sliders, and re-run until circles match droplets. "Detection strictness" is the main knob: lower finds more (including false positives), higher is more conservative.
